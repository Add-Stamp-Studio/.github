<p align="center"><em>Shoot or shootn’t?</em></p>

Add Stamp Studio is a software studio in Sofia, Bulgaria, that builds software for photo, video and
audio studios. We weave AI into how the software is used, so a studio’s work, from the first enquiry
to the final delivery, becomes clear steps that are easy to follow, extend and improve. We have built
ERP software since 2013 and work mostly in Europe, with studios and creators from all over the world
as well.

## What we make

**Hortun** · *in development*\
An AI-first ERP, CRM & BI for studios. Clients, sessions, galleries, the client’s selection and
delivery become one workflow. Hortun starts with photography and will grow into an AI-first ERP,
CRM & BI for photo, video and audio, with an assistant that can do anything its user can, with that
user’s permissions.
Ask about it at [shoot@addstamp.studio](mailto:shoot@addstamp.studio).

**[StampIt](https://stamp.bizarre.how/)** · *free and open source, MIT*\
Watermark a whole folder of photos right in the browser; not a single file leaves the computer.
Written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly. Source: [Add-Stamp-Studio/stampit](https://github.com/Add-Stamp-Studio/stampit).
